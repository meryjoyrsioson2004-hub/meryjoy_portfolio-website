Project details

AI / Research

CNN-Based Fermentation Monitoring and Optimization System for Quality Enhancement of Cacao Beans

Overview

This research focuses on developing a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)-based system for monitoring cacao bean fermentation and improving bean quality. The system combines image analysis and environmental monitoring to help identify fermentation stages and monitor conditions that may affect the quality of cacao beans. Through this approach, the study aims to provide a more consistent way of monitoring fermentation and support better decision-making during the process.

Problem & objective
Problem
  • Manual monitoring of cacao bean fermentation may lead to inconsistent assessments of fermentation progress and bean quality.
  • Changes in environmental conditions can affect the fermentation process and the quality of cacao beans.
  • Assessing fermentation stages consistently remains a challenge when relying on manual observation.
Objectives
  • To develop a CNN-based system for monitoring cacao bean fermentation.
  • To use image analysis to identify different fermentation stages.
  • To monitor environmental conditions that may affect cacao bean quality.
  • To provide recommendations that support more consistent fermentation monitoring and process improvement.
My role & contributions
Dataset Collection and Image Organization

Collected and organized cacao bean images for dataset preparation.

Image Preprocessing and Data Labeling

Assisted in preparing and labeling images for model training and evaluation.

Model Evaluation and Result Analysis

Evaluated model performance and analyzed the results to assess the system's effectiveness.

Research Documentation and Presentation

Contributed to documenting the research process, presenting findings, and preparing project materials.

Hardware & software
Hardware

Raspberry Pi 5, camera module, environmental sensors, fermentation chamber, exhaust fan, heater, and control components.

Software & Technologies

CNN-based image classification, image processing, IoT-based monitoring, and web-based dashboard.

System architecture
  1. 1. Data CollectionCacao bean images and environmental readings
  2. 2. Data ProcessingCollecting sensor readings and processing image inputs
  3. 3. CNN-Based Image ClassificationImage analysis to identify cacao fermentation stages
  4. 4. Monitoring and ControlMonitoring fermentation conditions and supporting process regulation
  5. 5. Web Dashboard and Data LoggingDisplaying monitoring results, status updates, and recorded data

The system integrates image analysis and environmental monitoring to assess cacao bean fermentation. It processes collected data, identifies fermentation stages, monitors fermentation conditions, and presents relevant information through a web-based dashboard. This simplified architecture illustrates the overall workflow of the system.

Implementation

The project involved preparing cacao bean image data for analysis and developing an AI-based approach to classify fermentation stages. The system combined image classification with environmental monitoring to support the assessment of fermentation progress. Model performance was then evaluated, and the results were documented to support the study's findings.

Testing & results

The system was evaluated through model performance analysis, sensor monitoring, and controlled fermentation trials. The evaluation focused on the accuracy of fermentation stage classification, the consistency of sensor readings, and the responsiveness of the monitoring and control functions. The results were analyzed to assess project performance and its potential to support more consistent cacao bean fermentation.
